### 3. ADVANCED DUPLICATE PREVENTION

Current issue:
same narrative repeating.

Examples:

* Ferrari EV variations
* Cybertruck variations
* ChatGPT traffic variations

NEW reviewer checks REQUIRED:

## Semantic duplication

same meaning even with different wording

## Narrative duplication

same story angle repeated

## Company saturation

same company dominating feed repeatedly

## Topic saturation

same trend repeated too frequently

Use:

* published_topics.json
* last 20 published articles
* semantic similarity checks

Reviewer should reject saturation automatically.
